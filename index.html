How does collection work at Pick Up Club?

Our method is simple. You put your bins ready inside or outside, we collect them, and you immediately receive clean, rinsed bins in return.

2/11

How often do you collect waste?

We collect waste according to an agreed schedule that suits your business. Additionally, we are available seven days a week to accommodate extra volumes.

3/11

In which regions are you active?

We provide nationwide waste collection across the Netherlands specifically tailored for the hospitality industry and SMEs.

4/11

Which waste streams do you collect?

We organise the collection of glass, cardboard, PD (plastic and drink cartons), swill, and residual waste to ensure efficient recycling.

5/11

What is allowed in the glass waste?

Bottle glass and packaging glass. Porcelain, pottery, stones, and flat glass do not belong here. Correct separation speeds up the recycling process.

6/11

What belongs with cardboard and paper?

Clean and dry cardboard and paper can go together. By keeping waste streams separate, the residual stream remains as small as possible.

7/11

What is PD waste?

PD stands for Plastic and Drink cartons. Separating these materials allows them to be efficiently processed and recycled into new products.

8/11

What is swill?

Swill consists of vegetable, fruit, and food remnants. Keeping this organic waste separate prevents contamination of other recyclable streams.

9/11

Do I always get clean bins back?

Yes, with every exchange you receive clean, rinsed bins so that your storage area remains neat and you can immediately continue your operations.

10/11

What if I have extra waste or my bins are full?

If you unexpectedly have full bins, we can immediately schedule an extra collection moment to ensure your site remains clean and functional.
